# Capability: Integration with AML ecosystem

**BRD Ref:** 12.12

## Business Requirement
System should have the ability to make available the CRR rating to other onboarding/compliance modules within and outside the platform

## Purpose
System capability to ensure that the customer risk rating is made available to all the consuming applications

## Outcome
Holistic view of the CRR results across the AML ecosystem in the enterprise


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Is the integration Real-Time (API) or Batch (File feed)?
- [ ] What happens if the receiving system (e.g. Onboarding) is down?
- [ ] Do we push scores or do they pull scores?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Integration endpoints not built (Target 26.3).

**System Behavior:** This requirement is handled by the core platform architecture, utilizing the standard React-Python stack. Specific behavior details are pending granular design.
