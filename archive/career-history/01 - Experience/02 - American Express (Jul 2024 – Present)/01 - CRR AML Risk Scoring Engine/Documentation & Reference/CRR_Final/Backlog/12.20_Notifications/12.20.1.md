# Sub-Requirement: 12.20.1

**Parent Capability:** [Notifications](12.20_Capability.md)

## Description
System must be able to send a notification to a pre-defined DL if there are significant events at any level (Enterprise wide, Market/Center, Product or Legal Entity level)

> Fluctuation in risk distribution - any change in risk rating distribution by more than X% (configurable by risk rating) in either direction.
