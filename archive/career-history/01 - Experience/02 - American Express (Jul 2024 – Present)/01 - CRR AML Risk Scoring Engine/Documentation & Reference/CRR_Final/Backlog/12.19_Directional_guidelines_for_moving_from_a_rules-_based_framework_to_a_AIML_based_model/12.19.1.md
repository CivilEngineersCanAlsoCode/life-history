# Sub-Requirement: 12.19.1

**Parent Capability:** [Directional guidelines for moving from a rules- based framework to a AI/ML based model](12.19_Capability.md)

## Description
POA state could consider moving to a AI/ML based model given the ability of such a solution to analyze complex patterns across the data attributes influencing CRR and adapt to evolving risk factors. Below are a few considerations:

1. Regulatory Compliance and Transparency: 
A. Explainability: The solution must be interpretable, i.e., it must be able to provide a detailed explanation of how the risk rating was generated.
B. Compliance Requirements: The solution must meet regulatory standards including guidelines on fairness, accountability and transparency.

2. Data Quality/Availability:
A. The data considerations highlighted elsewhere in the BRD must be solved as a pre-requisite. Historical CRR data, customer profiles and past risk outcomes would be required to train a effective model
B, Underlying data must span to cover all types of customers and risk scenarios to ensure ratings are not biased or inaccurate.

3. Integration: The solution must be integrated with existing applications related to customer onboarding, AML, TM etc., to ensure ongoing data ingestion.

4. Ongoing monitoring: The model must be continuously reviewed for performance, accuracy and fairness. Retraining/recalibrating maybe needed based on changes in customer behavior, market conditions or regulations.
