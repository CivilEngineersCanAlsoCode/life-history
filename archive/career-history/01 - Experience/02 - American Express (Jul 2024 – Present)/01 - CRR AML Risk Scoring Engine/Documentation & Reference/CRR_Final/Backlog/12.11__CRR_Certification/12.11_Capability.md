# Capability:  CRR Certification

**BRD Ref:** 12.11

## Business Requirement
System should have the capability to facilitate CRR certification

## Purpose
Ability to report out the current framework at any level and highlight updates made

## Outcome
Attest to the compliance of the framework with the regulatory requirements


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Does 'Certification' require a digitally signed record, or is a system log sufficient?
- [ ] Should certification reports be generated automatically on a schedule (e.g., Annual)?
- [ ] Do we need different certification templates for different regulators (e.g. RBI vs HKMA)?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Prioritized for next year's roadmap (2027).


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** This requirement is handled by the core platform architecture, utilizing the standard React-Python stack. Specific behavior details are pending granular design.
