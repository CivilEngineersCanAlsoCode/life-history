# Sub-Requirement: 12.9.1

**Parent Capability:** [Sandbox - Analysis of Results ](12.9_Capability.md)

## Description
System should be able to provide a graphic/visualization of the risk distribution changes due to an update.


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] What specific metrics define 'impact'? (e.g., % High Risk increase, specific segment shifts)

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Partially built (UI only). Integration with DAM/Rule Exec pending.


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** Analytical dashboards rendered in the UI (likely using a charting library like Recharts or D3). They consume aggregated metrics from the Reporting Database/Data Warehouse. They provide drill-down capabilities from Enterprise -> Market -> Legal Entity views.

**User Journey:** Dashboard -> Apply Filters (Date Range, Region) -> View Risk Distribution Pie Chart -> Drill down into 'High Risk' segment.
