# Capability: Risk Scoring - Customer Level

**BRD Ref:** 12.4

## Business Requirement
System should calculate the Risk Rating for each unique Customer ID

## Purpose
Enable the calculation of risk at the customer level

## Outcome
Consistent view of the customer risk in the ecosystem; taking into consideration all the variables that influence the risk assessment


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Is the 'Risk Rating' real-time or batch?
- [ ] Do we need to store the trace of HOW the score was calculated (lineage) for every customer?
- [ ] What is the precision required for the score (decimals vs integers)?

## Development Status

**Status:** Yes
**Comments:** Managed by Rule Execution Team.


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** The central engine of the CRR platform. It consumes Customer Data (Profile, Transaction, KYC) and applies the active Ruleset Configuration. It calculates Risk Points = Weight * Multiplier for each element, sums them, and maps the total to a Risk Class (Low/Med/High). This runs both in Batch (monthly/daily) and Real-time (onboarding).

**User Journey:** (Backend Process) No direct user interaction. Users view the *output* of this logic in the Customer 360 or Impact Analysis views.
