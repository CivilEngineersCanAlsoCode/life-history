# Sub-Requirement: 12.11.2

**Parent Capability:** [ CRR Certification](12.11_Capability.md)

## Description
Entitled users (contingent on access; permissions based) must be able to extract out all updates made to the CRR framework between 2 user selected dates or "as-of" date and extract the changes / snapshot of the CRR framework at the appropriate level (enterprise, center/market, legal entity) and be able to attest to the alignment of the framework to the corresponding regulatory requirements. As part of attestation, users should be able to upload supporting documents.
