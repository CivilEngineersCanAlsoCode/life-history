# Sub-Requirement: 12.15.2

**Parent Capability:** [Customer level CRR view / Report](12.15_Capability.md)

## Description
Users should be able to view a customer's risk rating (based on permissions) and zoom in to the underlying factors (risk category, risk elements, points, overrides) that contributed to the risk rating (each factor such as Product X, or Industry Y for Account Z etc., and its contribution to the risk calculation; this should include any manual overrides with corresponding details). The objective would be to have transparency into the risk rating designation. Access to this would be based on user permissions (should be configurable).


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] When 'zooming in' to underlying factors, do we need to show the exact rule version active at calculation time?
- [ ] How do we display contribution percentages for non-linear models or complex algorithms?
- [ ] Should manual override history include the 'approver' details and timestamp visible on the main view?
