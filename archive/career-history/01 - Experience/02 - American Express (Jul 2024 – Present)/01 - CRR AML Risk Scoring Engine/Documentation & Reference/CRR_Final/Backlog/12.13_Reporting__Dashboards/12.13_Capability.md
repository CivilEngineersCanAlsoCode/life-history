# Capability: Reporting / Dashboards

**BRD Ref:** 12.13

## Business Requirement
System should have capability to generate reports/dashboards to enable reviews and analytics

## Purpose
Ability to pull out reports with data attributes related to CRR framework as well as the resultant risk distribution of the customer base

## Outcome
Enabling comprehensive review and analysis of the CRR framework in the context of enterprise and regulatory policies


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Are these dashboards for operational monitoring (Job status) or Risk Analytics (Distribution)?
- [ ] What is the data latency acceptable for reports? (T+1 vs Real-time)
- [ ] Do we need Role-Based access to specific reports?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Managed by Core Reporting Team of GCIP.


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** This requirement is handled by the core platform architecture, utilizing the standard React-Python stack. Specific behavior details are pending granular design.
