# Sub-Requirement: 12.17.3

**Parent Capability:** [Data Considerations](12.17_Capability.md)

## Description
Data must be made available to support the risk element ratings calculations to ensure a high level of integrity in the risk scoring process


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] What constitutes 'high level of integrity'? Are there specific checksum or reconciliation reports required?
- [ ] Should the system flag 'stale' data attributes that haven't been updated in X months?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Data integration missing (Target 26.3).


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** Data Access Manager (DAM) serves as the integration layer. It acts as the data plumbing, fetching Customer Data from upstream systems (KYC, Transaction Lakes) and pushing generated Risk Ratings to downstream consumers (EDD, Investigations). It manages data lineage and quality checks.

**User Journey:** (Backend Process) Operations teams monitor Data Ingestion Reports to ensure feed health.
