# Capability: Notifications

**BRD Ref:** 12.20

## Business Requirement
System should have the ability to track and notify stakeholders about any significant events that relate to CRR

## Purpose
Real time notification whenever a significant event occurs in the CRR function (risk distribution changes)

## Outcome
Enabling the Business to be informed of critical issues immediately and respond appropriately


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Who defines a 'Significant Event'? (e.g., Score change > 10%)
- [ ] Should notifications be in-app, email, or webhook?
- [ ] Do we need an 'Inbox' for notifications within the platform?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Not developed yet (Target 2027).


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** This requirement is handled by the core platform architecture, utilizing the standard React-Python stack. Specific behavior details are pending granular design.
