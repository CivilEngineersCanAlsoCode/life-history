# Sub-Requirement: 12.12.4

**Parent Capability:** [Integration with AML ecosystem](12.12_Capability.md)

## Description
Data related to 'Alerts' should be available to the CRR scoring engine post disposition. The approach to ingesting these alerts and the applicable criteria would be defined separately for each category (EDD, SAR filing, Negative News, Sanctions etc.,). Product consideration: Once all the available data for each of the above category is documented, Business will provide feedback on the subset required for rule setup.


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] How do we handle 'Alert Storms' (1000s of alerts at once)?
- [ ] Do distinct alert categories (EDD vs Sanctions) trigger different scoring rules?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Integration endpoints not built (Target 26.3).

**System Behavior:** This requirement is handled by the core platform architecture, utilizing the standard React-Python stack. Specific behavior details are pending granular design.
