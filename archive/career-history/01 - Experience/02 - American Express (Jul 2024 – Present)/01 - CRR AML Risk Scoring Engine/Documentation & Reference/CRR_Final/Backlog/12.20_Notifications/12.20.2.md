# Sub-Requirement: 12.20.2

**Parent Capability:** [Notifications](12.20_Capability.md)

## Description
The notification must include the level at which the change was detected along the % breach with before and after values. It must also indicate the underlying reason for the breach (e.g., if a certain attribute was not populated at all or was at a different fill rate than usual).


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] How do we determine the 'underlying reason' algorithmically for complex multi-factor breaches?
- [ ] Should the notification email/message include deep links to the specific customer record?
- [ ] Are there sensitivity labels required on the notification content (e.g. 'Confidential')?
