# Sub-Requirement: 12.16.1

**Parent Capability:** [Rescoring capabilities  ](12.16_Capability.md)

## Description
System must be able to execute automated rescoring of the customer population based on the below frequency – 
> A monthly process to incorporate updates to transactional data and time based risk elements (e.g., length of relationship, date of establishment) that are not on the level of customer profile updates
> A daily delta process that incorporates updates to a customer’s profile


## Discovery Questions

- [ ] How do we handle 'Thrashing' if a customer's data changes multiple times a day?
