# Sub-Requirement: 12.4.1

**Parent Capability:** [Risk Scoring - Customer Level](12.4_Capability.md)

## Description
The risk rating should be calculated at the Customer level
1. Consumers: Each unique Customer ID (for e.g., "C360 Customer ID" in use currently) should have a risk rating. All accounts and sub accounts within the hierarchy of this Customer ID should be pulled in for calculation of the CRR.

2. Business: Each unique Customer ID (for e.g., "Obligor ID" in use currently) should have a risk rating. All accounts and sub accounts within the hierarchy of this business entity will need to be pulled in for calculation of the CRR.

3. Risk Rating at the Amex Legal Entity level - Each unique Customer ID that spans multiple legal entities should have a CRR calculated at the legal entity level (this will be required to meet regulatory obligations).
> Market consideration: The above should also apply to EMEA legal entities (AEESA, AEPE parent child relationship)

## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** The central engine of the CRR platform. It consumes Customer Data (Profile, Transaction, KYC) and applies the active Ruleset Configuration. It calculates Risk Points = Weight * Multiplier for each element, sums them, and maps the total to a Risk Class (Low/Med/High). This runs both in Batch (monthly/daily) and Real-time (onboarding).

**User Journey:** (Backend Process) No direct user interaction. Users view the *output* of this logic in the Customer 360 or Impact Analysis views.
