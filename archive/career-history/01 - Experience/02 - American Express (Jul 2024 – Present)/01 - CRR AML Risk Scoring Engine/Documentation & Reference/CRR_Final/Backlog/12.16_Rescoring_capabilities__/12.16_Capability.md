# Capability: Rescoring capabilities  

**BRD Ref:** 12.16

## Business Requirement
System should have the capability to rescore the customer population based on changes to underlying data and/or the CRR framework

## Purpose
Ability to apply both the CRR framework changes as well as changes in the underlying data to rescore the risk ratings for the customer population.

## Outcome
Enabling the second line to have a real time understanding of the risk distribution within the customer base.
