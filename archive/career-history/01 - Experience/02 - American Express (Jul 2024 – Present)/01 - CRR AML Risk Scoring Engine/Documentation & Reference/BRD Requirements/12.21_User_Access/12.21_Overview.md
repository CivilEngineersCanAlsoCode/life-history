# 12.21 User Access

**BRD Ref:** 12.21
**Status:** BLOCKING — IAM/Permissions not yet implemented
**Beads Task:** career-context-tv7

## Business Requirement
System should limit access to view, update and download functions based on permissions.

## Purpose
Enabling need-based access to view, update or download capability in the context of CRR related data.

## Outcome
Ensuring adequate controls over CRR framework and related data.

## CRITICAL NOTE
This capability is a BLOCKER for 6+ other capabilities (12.2.2, 12.2.3, 12.3.2, 12.6.4, 12.8.3, 12.9.4). Without IAM implementation, permission-based features across the system cannot function.
