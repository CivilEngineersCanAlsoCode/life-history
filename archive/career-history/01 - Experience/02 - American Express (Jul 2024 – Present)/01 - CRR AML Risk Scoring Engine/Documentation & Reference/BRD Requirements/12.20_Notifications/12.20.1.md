# 12.20.1 — Risk Distribution Fluctuation Notification

**Parent:** 12.20 Notifications
**Status:** Not Started

## Requirement
System must send notification to a pre-defined DL if there are significant events at any level (Enterprise wide, Market/Center, Product or Legal Entity level).

Trigger: Fluctuation in risk distribution — any change in risk rating distribution by more than X% (configurable by risk rating) in either direction.

## Coaching Notes
- Configurable threshold (X%) per risk rating band: Low (1-3), Medium (4-6), High (7-9), Prohibited (10)
- Pre-defined DL (distribution list) for notification recipients at each organizational level
- Fluctuation could be in either direction — sudden increase OR decrease in a risk band is significant
- Example: if Medium risk population drops 15% overnight, that could indicate a data feed failure, not genuine risk reduction

## Interview Notes
- Not Started — Target 2027
- The threshold-based approach prevents alert fatigue while catching genuine anomalies
