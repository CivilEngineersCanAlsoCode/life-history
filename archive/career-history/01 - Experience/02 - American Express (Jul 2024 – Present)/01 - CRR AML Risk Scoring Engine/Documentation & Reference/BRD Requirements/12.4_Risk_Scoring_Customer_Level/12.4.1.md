# 12.4.1 — Customer-Level Risk Rating Calculation

**Parent:** 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level
**Status:** Yes (Rule Execution Team)

## Requirement
The risk rating should be calculated at the Customer level:
1. **Consumers:** Each unique Customer ID (e.g., "C360 Customer ID") should have a risk rating. All accounts and sub-accounts within hierarchy pulled in for calculation.
2. **Business:** Each unique Customer ID (e.g., "Obligor ID") should have a risk rating. All accounts and sub-accounts within hierarchy pulled in.
3. **Legal Entity Level:** Each unique Customer ID spanning multiple legal entities should have CRR calculated at the legal entity level (regulatory obligation).
   - Market consideration: Also applies to EMEA legal entities (AEESA, AEPE parent-child relationship)

## Key Concept
Customer-level scoring (not account-level) is the core unit. All accounts roll up to the customer. This is critical because AML risk is assessed at the entity level, not the product level.

## Confirmed Details (from Coaching)
- **Final Customer Risk Score** = MAX(Pure Customer Score, Max Hierarchy Customer Score)
- This is the ONLY score sent to downstream systems — not account-level, not pure customer score
- Account-level risk score is being **decommissioned** from all downstream platforms
- Account score remains as an internal intermediate step in the 3-tier roll-up calculation
- Legal Entity level uses **Obligor ID** as the unique identifier

### Scoring Flow (confirmed in 12.1.4):
1. Rule-level: Multiplier resolution per triggered rule
2. Element-level: Risk Points = Weighting × Multiplier (MAX or MIN per element type)
3. Category-level: Sum of element risk points
4. Customer-level: 3-tier roll-up (Pure Customer vs Hierarchy) → MAX = Final Score
5. Risk Class: 1-3 Low, 4-6 Medium, 7-9 High, 10 Prohibited

## Interview Notes
- Customer-level is the strategic direction — account-level being phased out from downstream exposure
- Obligor ID is the legal entity-level identifier
