# 12.4.5 — Automatic "Very High" Classification

**Parent:** 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level
**Status:** Not Built (needs further business clarification)

## Requirement
System should be able to set CRR to "Very High" for specific scenarios (irrespective of calculated points/class). Rationale set by system based on scenario.

Example: If an intermediary partner is in a country with a very high geography corruption risk.

## Confirmed Details (from Coaching)

### Very High vs Prohibited:
| Classification | Trigger | Score | Status |
|---------------|---------|-------|--------|
| **Prohibited** | Sanctioned countries (NK, Cuba, Iran, Syria) or prohibited company structure | **10** | Built (configurable flags on risk elements) |
| **Very High** | High corruption geography (very high risk, but NOT sanctioned/prohibited) | **TBD (11? 9.5? not finalized)** | NOT built — needs business clarification |

### Key Characteristics:
- Both Very High and Prohibited **bypass normal risk calculation** — directly set the score
- Both are triggered when a specific risk element is satisfied for any account or customer
- Very High is a category **above High (7-9)** but **below Prohibited (10)**
- The exact numeric representation is NOT finalized
