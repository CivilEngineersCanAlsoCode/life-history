# BRD 12.11.1 — Periodic/Ad-Hoc Attestation

## Parent Requirement
12.11 — CRR Certification

## Status
Not Started

## Requirement
System must have ability to allow periodic or ad-hoc attestation of CRR framework (enterprise/center/market/legal entity level).

## Key Details
- Supports periodic scheduled attestation cycles
- Supports ad-hoc attestation on demand
- Attestation scope covers enterprise, center, market, and legal entity levels
- Facilitates compliance with regulatory attestation obligations

## Coaching Notes
- Not Started — Target 2027
- Related to audit trail (12.7) — attestation would leverage the version history and change tracking already built
- Attestation = formal sign-off that CRR framework is compliant with regulatory requirements at each level
- Pre-requisite: 12.7 audit trail and 12.13 reporting must be mature before attestation can be meaningful
