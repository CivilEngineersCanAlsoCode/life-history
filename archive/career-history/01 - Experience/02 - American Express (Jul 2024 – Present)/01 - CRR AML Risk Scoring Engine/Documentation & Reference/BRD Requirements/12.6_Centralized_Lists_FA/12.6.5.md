# 12.6.5 — Fundamental Assessment Y/N Question Sets

**Parent:** 12.6 Centralized Lists / FA
**Status:** Yes

## Requirement
The current approach of Fundamental Assessments being setup via a "Y/N question set" to compute the risk rating for the list values must be supported in the POA system.

## Key Concept
FA uses 10 ranked questions (rank 1-10). Each question is answered Y/N. The FA Score = rank of the highest "YES" answer. This score serves as a multiplier in risk rules.
