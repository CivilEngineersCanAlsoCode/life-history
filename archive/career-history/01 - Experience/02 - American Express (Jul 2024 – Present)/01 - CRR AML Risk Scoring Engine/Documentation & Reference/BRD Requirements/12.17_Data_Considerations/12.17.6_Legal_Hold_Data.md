# BRD 12.17.6 — Legal Hold Data

## Parent Requirement
12.17 — Data Considerations

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
Data related to Legal Hold (hold on record excluding from purge per record retention requirements) must be made available for CRR framework.

## Key Details
- Legal Hold data must be accessible to CRR framework
- Legal Hold: records excluded from purge per record retention requirements
- Ensures CRR calculations account for legal hold constraints
- Supports compliance with record retention policies

## Coaching Notes
- Legal Hold prevents record purge — these records must still be available for CRR scoring
- Compliance requirement: records under legal hold cannot be deleted but must be factored into risk calculations
- Relevant for FIU investigation scenarios where customer data is under preservation order
