# BRD 12.13.1 — Framework Scoring Matrix Report

## Parent Requirement
12.13 — Reporting / Dashboards

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
Report with all Risk Categories/Elements/Weightings at Enterprise/Center/Market/Product/Legal Entity. As-of reports. Change reports between dates.

## Key Details
- Includes all Risk Categories, Risk Elements, and Weightings
- Available at Enterprise, Center, Market, Product, and Legal Entity levels
- As-of reports (point-in-time snapshot)
- Change reports between user-selected dates

## Coaching Notes
- Maps to CRR Reports: **R12** (risk element trigger details), **R15** (implementation tracker), **R16** (scoring matrix snapshot)
- R16 is the most direct match — complete framework snapshot showing Categories, Elements, Weightings at all org levels
- R12 adds trigger-level detail — which specific rules fired for which customers
- R15 tracks sandbox lifecycle — implementation status, approval pipeline, version history
- As-of/change reports leverage the version history built in 12.7
