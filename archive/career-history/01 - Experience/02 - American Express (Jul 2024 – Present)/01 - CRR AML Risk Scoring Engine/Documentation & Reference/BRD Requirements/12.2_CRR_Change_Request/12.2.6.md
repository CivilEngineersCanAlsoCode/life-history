# 12.2.6 — Change Parameters Configuration

**Parent:** 12.2 CRR Change Request
**Status:** Not Started

## Requirement
They should have an option to set/select the following parameters:
- Applicable Market/Center, Legal Entity, Product for the change (one or more)
- Type of change including rationale and driver (e.g., regulatory, others — comments mandatory for 'others')

## Current Process (SharePoint Baseline)
- **Today:** Users fill these fields manually:
  - "Applicable Product Types" — free text (e.g., "All AENB Products", specific product codes)
  - "Applicable Location(s)" — free text (e.g., "US", "Germany and Austria", "Italy")
  - "Modification Reason" — dropdown: Best Practice / Regulatory Requirement / Examiner Expectation
  - "Modification Type" — dropdown: Add / Change / Remove
- **No multi-select structured fields:** Locations and products are typed as free text, not selected from validated lists

## What 12.2.6 Aims to Digitize
| Parameter | Current (SharePoint) | Target (Platform) |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Market/Center | Free text | Multi-select from configured markets |
| Legal Entity | Not captured separately | Dropdown from entity master |
| Product | Free text | Multi-select from product catalog |
| Modification Reason | 3-option dropdown | Expanded dropdown with mandatory comments for "Others" |
| Modification Type | 3-option dropdown | Carried forward (Add/Change/Remove) |

## SharePoint Production Data Insights
From 49 completed requests:
- **Regions span:** US, EMEA (DE/AT, IT, UK, ES), APAC, LACC, Global/Enterprise
- **Modification Reasons:** Best Practice most common, followed by Regulatory Requirement, then Examiner Expectation
- **Modification Types:** "Change" most frequent, then "Add", then "Remove"

## Interview Notes
- Capability NOT yet built — part of future roadmap (Target PI 26.4)
- Validated selectors (vs free text) will reduce submission errors and improve reporting/analytics on change requests
