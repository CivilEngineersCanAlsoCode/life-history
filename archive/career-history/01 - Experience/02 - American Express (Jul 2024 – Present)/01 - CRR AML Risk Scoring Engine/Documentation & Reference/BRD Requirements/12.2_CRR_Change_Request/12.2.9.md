# 12.2.9 — Request Withdrawal

**Parent:** 12.2 CRR Change Request
**Status:** Not Started

## Requirement
The submitting team/user should be able to cancel/withdraw the request. A notification must be sent out to the CRR team when this occurs.

## Current Process (SharePoint Baseline)
- **Today:** No formal withdrawal mechanism — user would email GFCC-RMO team or manually update the SharePoint entry
- **No notification:** CRR team may not be aware a request was withdrawn unless manually informed
- **Soft delete:** Rows remain in SharePoint list; status may be updated to indicate cancellation

## What 12.2.9 Aims to Digitize
- Self-service "Withdraw Request" action available to the original submitter
- Automated notification to CRR team on withdrawal (with reason if provided)
- Withdrawal should be allowed at any status before "Implemented"
- Soft delete: Request remains in system for audit trail (not permanently removed)
- Withdrawn requests should be filterable/searchable for reporting
