# BRD 12.15.3 — Risk Rating Change History Report

## Parent Requirement
12.15 — Customer Level CRR View / Report

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
Report highlighting risk rating changes to customer within user timeframe (attribute changes, before/after, who/when/what).

## Key Details
- User-defined timeframe for report generation
- Highlights all risk rating changes for a customer
- Shows attribute-level changes (before and after values)
- Captures who made the change
- Captures when the change occurred
- Captures what was changed

## Coaching Notes
- Builds on 12.7 audit trail and 12.16 rescoring — tracks how customer scores changed over time
- Must distinguish between: framework-driven changes (rule/weight updates) vs data-driven changes (customer attribute updates) vs manual overrides
- User-defined timeframe allows focused investigation (e.g., "what changed for this customer in last 6 months?")
