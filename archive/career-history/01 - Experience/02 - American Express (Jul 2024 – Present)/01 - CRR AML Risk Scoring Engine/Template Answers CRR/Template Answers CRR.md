# Template Answers CRR - Index

This folder contains 15 individual template answer markdown files for the CRR (Customer Risk Rating) AML Risk Scoring Engine project at American Express, plus combined and brain dump files.

## Individual Answer Files (01-15)

1. **01. Problem Definition (Clarity Test).md** - What exact problem? For whom? Pain intensity? What if unsolved? Why urgent?
2. **02. Customer & Persona Depth.md** - Primary user? Day in life? KPIs? Frustrations? Previous tools? Constraints?
3. **03. Discovery & Validation.md** - How validated? What surprised? Why this solution? Where wrong?
4. **04. Solution Architecture & Trade-offs.md** - Build choices? Alternatives considered? Trade-offs? Technical risks?
5. **05. Metrics & North Star.md** - North star metric? Leading/lagging indicators? How measured impact?
6. **06. AI/ML Depth.md** - AI component? Why this approach? Risks? Fallbacks?
7. **07. Scalability & Reliability.md** - How scales? First to break? Infra limits? Multi-tenant? Privacy/compliance?
8. **08. Monetization & Business Impact.md** - Revenue impact? Business case? Cost-to-build vs value?
9. **09. Stakeholder Management.md** - Biggest pushback? Engineering constraints? Sales/legal blockers?
10. **10. Execution & Delivery.md** - How prioritized? What slipped? Mistakes made and recovery?
11. **11. Competition & Differentiation.md** - Competitive landscape? Why customers would choose? Defensibility?
12. **12. UX & Product Thinking.md** - How reduced cognitive load? Journey design? Usability measured?
13. **13. Failure Mode Analysis.md** - Most likely failure? Hidden assumptions? Adoption risks?
14. **14. Product Strategy & Future Vision.md** - Strategic direction? Platform vision? Future roadmap?
15. **15. Personal Ownership Filter.md** - What you personally owned? What without you fails? Hardest part?
