## 14. Product Strategy & Future Vision

- What was your product strategy and roadmap?
- What is the multi-year vision?
- How are you thinking about next-gen capabilities?
- What's the north star for the next 3 years?

**What was the product strategy and roadmap?**
**Strategy**: Transform compliance from "operational cost center" to "strategic advantage" by making rule configuration self-service, explainable, and auditable. Phase the modernization into independent, value-delivering capabilities that build on each other and reduce regulatory risk at each step. **Phase 1 (delivered, PI 1–2)**: Sandbox testing + self-service rule configuration UI (immediate user value, foundational for future work), advanced rule composition (enables complex logic without code), centralized list management (eliminates manual overrides), maker-checker controls + hardened audit trails (regulatory table-stakes) — 7 of 10 Rally capabilities delivered. **Phase 2 (in progress)**: Full Cadence deprecation + production cutover, remaining capabilities, within the 3-year regulatory timeline. **12-month roadmap (next 6 months)**: (1) **Real-time rule impact simulation** — analysts can "what-if" scenarios before deployment; (2) **Advanced monitoring and anomaly detection** — dashboard showing rule performance, score distribution changes, and risk category evolution; (3) **Regulatory reporting automation** — generate audit-ready compliance reports automatically; (4) **API-first third-party integrations** — allow KYC, TM, EDD teams to embed GCIP scoring in their workflows.

**What is the multi-year vision?**
**3-year vision**: GCIP becomes the **"compliance operating system" for Amex**, enabling every team touching customer risk (compliance, fraud, KYC, regulatory) to make decisions from a single, explainable, auditable source of truth. **Year 1 (current)**: Finish self-service rule configuration and sandbox capabilities; establish GCIP as the trusted scoring platform. **Year 2**: Introduce explainability-first features (scoring rationale, rule impact simulation) and regulatory reporting automation. Explore interpretable ML for anomaly detection (directional per BRD 12.19); **Year 3**: Transition toward hybrid human-in-the-loop AI/ML scoring for specific risk categories; establish regulatory partnerships on responsible AI/AML. The vision is NOT "replace human compliance with AI" but "augment human expertise with AI-powered insights that maintain regulatory transparency."

**How are you thinking about next-gen capabilities?**
Three directions: (1) **Explainability-as-a-feature** — move beyond "here's your risk score" to "here's why, which rules fired, which didn't, what would change the score." This requires decomposing the risk score into explainable components. Regulatory benefit: satisfies FinCEN requirements for scoring transparency; User benefit: analysts can explain scores to customers and auditors; (2) **Predictive rule optimization** — use historical rule performance data to suggest rule changes. E.g., "Your current rule flags 5% of customers as High Risk, but 99% pass AML review. Consider relaxing this rule." This uses ML (historical data analysis) to optimize human-authored rules, maintaining explainability; (3) **Continuous risk monitoring** — instead of daily/monthly rescoring, monitor customer risk continuously (streaming updates from 30+ sources trigger rescoring in real-time). This enables faster response to emerging threats (e.g., a sanctioned entity acquisition triggers instant scoring update).

**What's the north star for the next 3 years?**
**North Star: Regulatory confidence in explainable, auditable, continuously-monitored AML risk scoring**. Measured by: (1) **Zero AML audit findings** related to risk scoring explainability or auditability (across internal and external regulatory audits); (2) **Regulatory partnership milestone** — become a reference implementation for responsible AI/AML among peer payment processors, influencing regulatory expectations; (3) **Operational leverage** — enable Amex to scale customer acquisition [VERIFY: 3×] without proportional compliance headcount increases (through automation and self-service); (4) **Industry recognition** — position GCIP's explainability and compliance design as a differentiator (case studies, speaking engagements at AML conferences, thought leadership on responsible fintech compliance). **Key metrics**: Regulatory audit findings (target: zero), days-to-respond to new regulatory guidance (target: 1–2 days), analyst time-to-configure (target: [VERIFY: <1 hour] from request to approval), downstream consumer satisfaction (NPS target: [VERIFY: >70]), and industry benchmarking (position Amex as top-quartile on compliance automation among peer banks).
