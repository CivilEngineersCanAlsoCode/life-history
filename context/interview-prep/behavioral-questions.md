# Behavioral Interview Question Templates

## Usage
Har question ke liye STAR format mein jawab prepare karo.
Life Brain se examples nikalne ke liye: `search("leadership examples at Sprinklr")`

---

## Leadership & Influence

1. **Tell me about a time you led a team through a difficult situation.**
   - Situation: What was the challenge? Stakes kya the?
   - Task: Tumhari specific responsibility kya thi?
   - Action: Kya kiya — exactly, step by step?
   - Result: Kya hua? Metrics? What changed?

2. **Describe a time you influenced stakeholders without direct authority.**
   - Who were the stakeholders? Kya interests the unke?
   - Kaunsi information/data use ki persuade karne ke liye?
   - Opposition kaise handle ki?

3. **Give an example of when you set a direction others disagreed with.**
   - Why did you believe in your direction?
   - How did you handle pushback?
   - Outcome kya tha?

---

## Problem Solving

4. **Tell me about the most complex problem you've solved.**
   - Problem define karo clearly: root cause kya tha?
   - What alternatives did you consider?
   - How did you measure success?

5. **Describe a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.**
   - What was the urgency?
   - What data did you have? What was missing?
   - How did you mitigate the risk of being wrong?

6. **Give an example of a creative solution you developed.**
   - Why was the standard approach insufficient?
   - Where did the idea come from?
   - How did you validate it before full commitment?

---

## Execution & Delivery

7. **Tell me about a project you delivered under tight deadline/resource constraints.**
   - What was the original scope vs what you delivered?
   - What tradeoffs did you make?
   - What would you do differently?

8. **Describe a time you had to reprioritize mid-project.**
   - What triggered the change?
   - How did you communicate it?
   - What was the impact on the team?

9. **Give an example of a time you failed to meet expectations.**
   - What were the expectations? Why did you fall short?
   - What was the impact?
   - What did you learn and change after?

---

## Collaboration & Conflict

10. **Tell me about a time you had a significant conflict with a teammate or manager.**
    - What was the root of the conflict?
    - How did you approach resolution?
    - What's your relationship like now?

11. **Describe a time you gave difficult feedback to someone.**
    - Why was it difficult?
    - How did you prepare and deliver it?
    - How did they receive it?

12. **Give an example of when you had to work with a difficult person.**
    - What made them difficult?
    - What did you try? What worked?
    - What did you learn about working with different styles?

---

## Learning & Growth

13. **Tell me about something significant you learned in the last year.**
    - What prompted the learning?
    - How did you approach learning it?
    - How have you applied it?

14. **Describe a time you received critical feedback that changed how you work.**
    - Who gave it? What was the feedback?
    - Why did it land/stick?
    - What changed specifically?

15. **Give an example of when you stepped outside your comfort zone.**
    - What was the risk?
    - How did you prepare?
    - What was the result?

---

## STAR Story Checklist

Before every answer, verify:
- [ ] Situation is specific (not vague/generic)
- [ ] Your role is clear (not "we did everything")
- [ ] Actions are detailed — not just "I communicated" but HOW
- [ ] Results have numbers where possible
- [ ] Story is <3 minutes when spoken
- [ ] No confidential/sensitive information included

## Salary Negotiation Anchor Points

```
Research first:
- Glassdoor/LinkedIn/levels.fyi for role + location + level
- Network: ask 2-3 people in similar roles

Opening moves:
- Never give first number if asked ("What's your current CTC?")
- Response: "I'm flexible, but based on market data for this role, I'm targeting [X]"
- Always negotiate: 90% of offers have room

Typical levers:
1. Base salary
2. Signing bonus (one-time, often more room)
3. Equity / RSU refresh
4. Title/level
5. Start date, remote flexibility

Close:
- Get it in writing before giving notice
- Ask for 48-72 hours to review (always)
```
