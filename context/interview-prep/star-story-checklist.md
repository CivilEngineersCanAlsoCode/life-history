# STAR Story Checklist & Bank Template

## STAR Format Quick Reference

| Component | What It Covers | Target Length |
|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| **S**ituation | Context — what was happening, when, where | 1-2 sentences |
| **T**ask | Your specific responsibility in this situation | 1 sentence |
| **A**ction | What YOU did — step by step, your decisions | 60-70% of story |
| **R**esult | What changed — quantified if possible | 1-2 sentences |

---

## Story Template

```markdown
## Story: [Title — e.g., "CGB Launch Under 6-Week Deadline"]

### Category
[ ] Leadership  [ ] Problem Solving  [ ] Execution  [ ] Collaboration  [ ] Learning

### Situation
[2 sentences max. Set the stage.]
Company / project / time period / stakes.

### Task
[1 sentence. YOUR role only.]
I was responsible for ___

### Action
[Most important part. Be specific — no "we" unless explaining group then YOUR part.]
1. First, I ___
2. Then, I ___
3. When X happened, I decided to ___ because ___
4. I also ___

### Result
[Quantify. Company impact, personal growth, relationship outcome.]
- Metric 1: ___
- Metric 2: ___
- Qualitative outcome: ___

### Questions This Story Answers
- "Tell me about a time you ___"
- "Describe a time you ___"
- "Give an example of ___"

### Potential Follow-ups & Your Answers
Q: What would you do differently?
A: ___

Q: What did you learn?
A: ___

Q: What was the hardest part?
A: ___
```

## Pre-Interview Review Checklist

**48 hours before:**
- [ ] Practice top 5 stories out loud (not in head)
- [ ] Time each story — should be 2-3 min spoken
- [ ] Update any metrics with latest numbers
- [ ] Remove any confidential specifics

**Day of:**
- [ ] Review 2-3 company-specific research points
- [ ] Confirm stories cover: leadership, failure, conflict, impact, learning
- [ ] Have 3 good questions ready for interviewer
- [ ] Know who's interviewing you — LinkedIn check

## Common Weaknesses (Be Honest But Strategic)

```
Strong "weakness" answers follow this pattern:
1. Real weakness (not strength-disguised: "I work too hard")
2. Context of where it showed up
3. What you did about it
4. Evidence it's improving

Example: "Historically I'd try to solve problems alone before asking for help.
In the CGB project I realized that held us back. I now schedule explicit check-ins
at 30% and 70% completion to surface blockers early. Team velocity improved as a result."
```

## Questions to Ask Interviewer

**About the role:**
- "What does success look like in the first 90 days?"
- "What's the biggest challenge the person in this role will face?"
- "How do you measure impact for this role?"

**About the team:**
- "How does the team handle disagreement?"
- "What's the on-call / incident culture like?"
- "What's something you wish you knew before joining?"

**About growth:**
- "How has someone in this role grown in the past 2 years?"
- "What skills does the team most need to develop?"

**Company / product:**
- "What's the biggest risk to the company's strategy right now?"
- "How has the product evolved in the last year?"
