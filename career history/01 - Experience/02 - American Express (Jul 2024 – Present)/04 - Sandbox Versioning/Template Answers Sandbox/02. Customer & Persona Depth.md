## Q2. Customer & Persona Depth

**Who are the actual users of this feature, and what are their workflows and pain points?**

Primary users: Compliance Analysts and Compliance Managers (the compliance team) across 40+ markets. They manage CRR rules, asset configurations, and Fundamental Assessment criteria. Secondary users: MLRO (Money Laundering Reporting Officer) teams and MCO (Market Compliance Officer) teams, with view-only access to sandbox results (BRD 12.9.4, default ON). These personas span compliance, risk, and regulatory domains—not technologists. They require clear, domain-specific language and low cognitive load for high-stakes decisions.

**What is each persona trying to accomplish, and where do they struggle?**

Compliance analysts need to update risk rules in response to regulatory guidance, customer onboarding changes, or operational incidents. They struggle because: (a) no sandbox exists to test changes without hitting production, (b) no version history to audit changes or revert mistakes, (c) no simulation capability to forecast impact on customer risk classifications, (d) no audit trail for regulatory exams. Compliance managers oversee these analysts and must ensure BRD 12.10 compliance (maker-checker governance)—currently a manual, error-prone process.

**How do they measure success? What are their KPIs?**

Analysts measure success by: time-to-implement a rule change (currently [VERIFY: add baseline]), confidence in test results before production promotion, speed of rollback if issues surface, audit trail completeness for regulatory review. Managers measure by: zero instances of single-actor configuration changes reaching production, cycle time from change request to approval, audit trail export time for exams.

**What is their relationship to adjacent teams (IT, MLRO, GCP platform)?**

Analysts submit change requests but depend on IT for rollbacks. MLRO teams currently have no view into sandbox results—they rubber-stamp approvals blindly. GCP platform owns the infrastructure and provides APIs for stateless sandbox execution. Close coordination required: API stability, simulation performance (SLA [VERIFY: add target]), data masking for production copy.

**What does "done" look like for this persona?**

One-click sandbox creation, drag-drop rule/asset edits, instant simulation preview, two-click approval chain, automated promotion with rollback capability, and exportable audit trail for exams. All within the CRR UI—no context-switching to GCP or manual IT tickets.
