## Q5. Metrics & North Star

**What is the north star metric for this feature?**

100% of CRR configuration changes (Rules, Assets, Fundamental Assessment) flow through the Unified Sandbox Journey with zero production incidents attributable to misconfigured rules post-launch. This metric proves: (1) adoption is complete (users trust the sandbox), (2) quality is high (no bugs leaking to production), (3) the feature delivers on its core promise (controlled change management).

Audit metric: audit trail export completeness (100% of actions logged with who/what/when/why).

**What are the leading indicators of product-market fit?**

Analyst teams proactively create sandboxes for experimental changes (not just mandatory updates). Simulation results are reviewed before approval (not rubber-stamped). Rollback is used in response to production issues (validates that users know how to use it). MLRO teams provide meaningful approvals/rejections (not perfunctory approvals).

**How do you measure regulatory compliance?**

Audit trail completeness: every change has documented justification, two approvals from different users, timestamp, and status. Maker-checker adherence: 100% of promoted changes have two distinct approvers. Simulation audit: every promoted change has a corresponding simulation run with recorded results. These metrics are not optional—they're compliance proof points for BRD 12.8, 12.10, and audit requirements.

**How do you report on this to stakeholders?**

Weekly dashboard: adoption rate, cycle time, approval turnaround, incident count. Quarterly review: long-term KPI progress against baseline.
