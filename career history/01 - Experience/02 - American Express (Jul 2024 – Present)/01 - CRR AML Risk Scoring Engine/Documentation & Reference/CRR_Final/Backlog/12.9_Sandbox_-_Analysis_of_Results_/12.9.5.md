# Sub-Requirement: 12.9.5

**Parent Capability:** [Sandbox - Analysis of Results ](12.9_Capability.md)

## Description
System should have the ability to show a direct comparison of Current Production Risk Ratings vs Sandbox Ratings (using the underlying data at the same point in time). This should be available at various levels > zoom in from a global/enterprise view to a particular center/market, legal entity level or product level (and combinations thereof). This should available for download as a spreadsheet/pdf.
