# Capability: Sandbox - Analysis of Results 

**BRD Ref:** 12.9

## Business Requirement
System should provide the ability for users to review results from simulation exercises for better decision making and analytics

## Purpose
Ability to validate the results of simulations prior to production implementation

## Outcome
Comprehensive view of the impact of any change on the customer population


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Should users be able to drill down from aggregate stats to individual customer examples?
- [ ] Do we need visual charts (histograms, pie charts) for the distribution?
- [ ] Can results be exported to Tableau/PowerBI?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Partially built (UI only). Integration with DAM/Rule Exec pending.


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** Analytical dashboards rendered in the UI (likely using a charting library like Recharts or D3). They consume aggregated metrics from the Reporting Database/Data Warehouse. They provide drill-down capabilities from Enterprise -> Market -> Legal Entity views.

**User Journey:** Dashboard -> Apply Filters (Date Range, Region) -> View Risk Distribution Pie Chart -> Drill down into 'High Risk' segment.
