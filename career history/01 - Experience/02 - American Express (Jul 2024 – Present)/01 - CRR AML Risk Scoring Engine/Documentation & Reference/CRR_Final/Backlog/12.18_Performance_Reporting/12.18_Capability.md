# Capability: Performance Reporting

**BRD Ref:** 12.18

## Business Requirement
System must have the capability to track and report on the performance of the CRR application

## Purpose
Ensuring that the data that is sourced for purposes of CRR is accurate as well as exhaustive

## Outcome
Providing Business a high degree of confidence that the risk rating calculated by the system reflects the true risk of the customer


## Discovery Questions

- [ ] Do we need to report on 'Business Value' metrics? (e.g. Risk distribution stability)
- [ ] Who is the audience for these reports? (IT Ops vs Risk Business)
