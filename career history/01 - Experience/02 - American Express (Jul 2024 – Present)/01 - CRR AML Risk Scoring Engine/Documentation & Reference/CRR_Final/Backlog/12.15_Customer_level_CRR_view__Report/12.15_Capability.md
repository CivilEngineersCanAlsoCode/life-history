# Capability: Customer level CRR view / Report

**BRD Ref:** 12.15

## Business Requirement
System should be able to provide the risk rating for a particular customer as well as make available the contributing factors that resulted in that rating

## Purpose
Providing visibility into the factors that contributed to a particular customer's risk rating as well tracking the changes that caused any rescoring to an updated rating

## Outcome
Increase in productivity by reducing the time that is taken currently in researching the risk rating and the underlying factors contributing to it


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] Should the 'Customer Report' show the history of risk rating changes for that customer?
- [ ] Can this report be downloaded as a PDF for audit evidence?
