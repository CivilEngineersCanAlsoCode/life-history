# Capability: Data Considerations

**BRD Ref:** 12.17

## Business Requirement
System implementation plan must solve for the data related challenges in the current state to ensure integrity in the CRR process

## Purpose
Ensuring that the data that is sourced for purposes of CRR is accurate as well as exhaustive

## Outcome
Providing Business a high degree of confidence that the risk rating calculated by the system reflects the true risk of the customer


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] What specific 'data related challenges' exist today? (e.g. Missing fields, Stale data, Duplicates)
- [ ] How does the system handle 'null' values in critical fields during risk calculation?
- [ ] Do we need a data quality dashboard to flag issues *before* scoring runs?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Data integration missing (Target 26.3).


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** Data Access Manager (DAM) serves as the integration layer. It acts as the data plumbing, fetching Customer Data from upstream systems (KYC, Transaction Lakes) and pushing generated Risk Ratings to downstream consumers (EDD, Investigations). It manages data lineage and quality checks.

**User Journey:** (Backend Process) Operations teams monitor Data Ingestion Reports to ensure feed health.
