## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** Data Access Manager (DAM) serves as the integration layer. It acts as the data plumbing, fetching Customer Data from upstream systems (KYC, Transaction Lakes) and pushing generated Risk Ratings to downstream consumers (EDD, Investigations). It manages data lineage and quality checks.

**User Journey:** (Backend Process) Operations teams monitor Data Ingestion Reports to ensure feed health.
