# Sub-Requirement: 12.17.6

**Parent Capability:** [Data Considerations](12.17_Capability.md)

## Description
Data that is related to "Legal Hold" (refers to the hold on a record that excludes it from any purge process per record retention requirements) must be made available so that it can be leveraged as needed for the CRR framework


## Discovery Questions
- [ ] What is the mechanism to identify 'Legal Hold' status on a record? (Flag in source system?)
- [ ] Are there specific encryption requirements for Legal Hold data exports?
- [ ] Does Legal Hold data need to be segregated physically or just logically?

## Development Status

**Status:** No
**Comments:** Data integration missing (Target 26.3).


## System Behavior & User Journey

**System Behavior:** Data Access Manager (DAM) serves as the integration layer. It acts as the data plumbing, fetching Customer Data from upstream systems (KYC, Transaction Lakes) and pushing generated Risk Ratings to downstream consumers (EDD, Investigations). It manages data lineage and quality checks.

**User Journey:** (Backend Process) Operations teams monitor Data Ingestion Reports to ensure feed health.
