# BRD 12.11.2 — Change Snapshot & Attestation

## Parent Requirement
12.11 — CRR Certification

## Status
Not Started

## Requirement
Entitled users must extract all updates between 2 user-selected dates or as-of date and extract changes/snapshot at appropriate level. Must be able to attest alignment with regulatory requirements. Users should upload supporting documents.

## Key Details
- Extract updates between two user-selected dates
- Extract changes/snapshot as-of a specific date
- Snapshot available at appropriate organizational level
- Users can attest alignment with regulatory requirements
- Supporting document upload capability required

## Coaching Notes
- Not Started — Target 2027
- Change snapshot between dates maps to version history (12.7.2) and reporting (R16 Scoring Matrix, R13 FA/Lists changes)
- Current sandbox History tab already tracks who/when/what — snapshot attestation would formalize this for regulatory purposes
- Document upload = new capability not present in current system
