# 12.20 Notifications

**BRD Ref:** 12.20
**Status:** Not Started (Target 2027)
**Beads Task:** career-context-a43

## Business Requirement
System should have the ability to track and notify stakeholders about any significant events that relate to CRR.

## Purpose
Real-time notification whenever a significant event occurs in CRR function.

## Outcome
Enable Business to be informed of critical issues immediately and respond appropriately.

## Coaching Context

### Connection to CRR Application Architecture:
- **Alerts** is the **6th tab** in the CRR application (from 12.3 coaching) — currently Not Started
- Notifications would surface significant events directly in the CRR UI
- Also requires email/DL-based notification for stakeholders not actively in the application

### What Would Trigger Notifications:
- Risk distribution fluctuation beyond configurable threshold (X% in either direction)
- Detected at any level: Enterprise, Market, Product, Legal Entity
- Could be caused by: framework changes (rule updates), data changes (data quality issues), or external events

### Related Capabilities:
- 12.16 (Rescoring) produces the risk distribution data that notifications would monitor
- 12.18.1 Reports 1-4 (data quality) could also trigger notifications on data pipeline failures
- 12.13.3 (Risk Distribution Dashboard) provides the visual view; notifications provide the proactive alert
