# BRD 12.12.1 — Downstream System Integration

## Parent Requirement
12.12 — Integration with AML Ecosystem

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
System must make Risk Rating available to downstream systems (KYC, AML Investigations, TM, EDD, Anomaly Index calc etc.).

## Key Details
- Risk Rating exposed to KYC systems
- Risk Rating exposed to AML Investigations
- Risk Rating exposed to Transaction Monitoring (TM)
- Risk Rating exposed to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
- Risk Rating exposed to Anomaly Index calculation
- Additional downstream consumers as identified

## Coaching Notes
- The **Final Customer Risk Score** = MAX(Pure, Hierarchy) is the ONLY score exposed downstream (from 12.4 coaching)
- Account-level scoring is being decommissioned — downstream systems will receive customer-level scores
- Integration pattern: CRR scoring results → BigQuery → downstream system consumption
- Obligor ID used as legal entity identifier for downstream correlation
