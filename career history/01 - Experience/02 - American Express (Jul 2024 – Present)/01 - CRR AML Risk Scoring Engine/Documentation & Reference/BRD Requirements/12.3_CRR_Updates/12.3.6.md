# 12.3.6 — Weighting Override Levels

**Parent:** 12.3 CRR Updates
**Status:** Partially built (Market-level exists; Legal Entity/Product overrides missing)

## Requirement
The Risk Element weighting should be at the enterprise level by default with the ability to have a local override at:
- Enterprise
- Center specific (Markets with unique combinations, e.g., "International Products - D2")
- Legal Entity
- Product

## Key Concept
Hierarchical override model — Enterprise sets defaults, markets can customize. Balances central governance with local autonomy.

## Confirmed Details (from Coaching)

### Override Hierarchy:
```
Enterprise (default weighting)
  └── Market Override (geographic market where Amex does business)
       └── Legal Entity Override (future)
            └── Product Override (future — most specific, highest priority)
```
- Most specific override wins
- If no override exists at a level → inherits from parent level

### POD → POA Terminology Shift:
| POD (Cadence — old) | POA (CRR 2.0 — new) |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| **Center** — customizable grouping, business-defined (e.g., could put international card products in US as separate center) | **Market** — standardized geographic market where Amex does business |
| Flexible but inconsistent | Standardized across enterprise |

### Market Settings Configuration:
Inside sandbox, user configures per-element market settings:
- **Default multiplier** (value-based or FA-based)
- **Applicability** (whether element is active for that scope)
- **Weighting** (can be overridden at each level)

### Current Status:
- **Built:** Enterprise-level weighting + Market-level override (via market sandbox → market settings → localization)
- **How market override works:** When user creates a market-level sandbox and updates the weighting in market settings, this localizes the risk element and creates a market-level weighting override
- **Missing:** Legal Entity-specific and Product-specific overrides
- **Timeline:** Will be built as enhancement after Phase 2 / MVP 2 completion — not in immediate roadmap

### Production Data Context:
- Weight 0 = deactivated (Repat/Brexit elements)
- Weight 4000 = HRO override (auto-score 9)
- ARF weights: Low (~300), Medium (~600), High (~900) — cumulative
- Normal element weights: vary by risk significance
