# 12.3.8 — Product-Specific Framework Customization

**Parent:** 12.3 CRR Updates
**Status:** In Development

## Requirement
For PayFac, Intermediaries, GNS, etc. — system must provide the ability to customize the CRR framework. Need to ensure system has the data to risk rate these specific products.

## Confirmed Details (from Coaching)

### Product-Specific Context:
- **PayFac (Payment Facilitators):** Merchants processing payments through Amex
- **Intermediaries:** Third-party entities in payment chain
- **GNS (Global Network Services):** Partner networks using Amex infrastructure
- Each product type has unique risk characteristics requiring customized scoring

### How Product Customization Works:
- Risk framework can be customized at the **Product level** within the override hierarchy:
  ```
  Enterprise → Center → Legal Entity → Product (most specific)
  ```
- Product-specific elements, weightings, and rules can be configured
- Simulation results show comparison grouped by Center × Product × Legal Entity × Risk Score
- MCOs can see product-specific risk distribution in results dashboard

### Data Availability Challenge:
- Different products have different data points available for scoring
- PayFac transactions have merchant-level data that card member data doesn't have
- GNS partner scoring uses geography corruption risk elements (seen in production data: 4000-point weight)
- System must handle cases where a data point exists for one product but not another

### Connection to Other Sub-Requirements:
- 12.3.6 (Weighting Overrides) enables product-level weighting customization
- 12.3.7 (Local Elements) enables product-specific risk elements
- 12.3.4/12.3.5 (Enterprise/Local) determines how product customizations cascade or remain local

## Interview Notes
- Product-specific customization is critical for Amex's diverse product portfolio
- Data availability varies significantly by product type — system must be flexible
- GNS Partner scoring already active in production with specialized elements (e.g., geography corruption risk at weight 4000)
