# BRD 12.14.2 — Market-Specific View

## Parent Requirement
12.14 — User Interface / Experience

## Status
Yes

## Requirement
Select specific market and render framework. Side-by-side/overlay for easy deviation understanding from enterprise framework.

## Key Details
- Select and render framework for a specific market
- Side-by-side comparison view available
- Overlay view available
- Facilitates easy understanding of deviations from enterprise framework
- Cross-market scanning capability

## Coaching Notes
- Enterprise vs Market comparison built in CRR tab
- Market overrides visible via sandbox localization (12.3.6) — markets can customize weightings within enterprise-defined min/max
- Side-by-side view shows where a market deviates from enterprise defaults
- POD used "Center" (customizable, business-defined), POA uses "Market" (standardized geographic)
