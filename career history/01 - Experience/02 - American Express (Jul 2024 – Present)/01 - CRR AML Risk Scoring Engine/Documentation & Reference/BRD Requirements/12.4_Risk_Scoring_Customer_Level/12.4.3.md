# 12.4.3 — Manual Risk Rating Override

**Parent:** 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level
**Status:** Not Built

## Requirement
Users must be able to override the risk rating for a customer or a target population (from current rating to Low/Medium/High/Very High):
- System should require a justification (mandatory)
- Users should be able to directly set the target population via an Excel upload of customers
- The ability to override would be permission-based (configurable)

## Confirmed Details (from Coaching)

### Override Characteristics:
- **Permanent flag** — once set, stays forever (does NOT reset on next CRR recalculation)
- Must be taken into account for all future risk calculations for that customer
- Moving to **customer-level override** (not account-level) going forward

### Who Can Override:
- **FIU (Financial Investigation Unit)** — when they find something suspicious during investigation
- **MCO (Market Compliance Officer)** — when reviewing a case and identifying elevated risk
- Permission-based — only authorized users can set overrides

### Override Scenarios:
- Investigation reveals suspicious activity not captured by rules
- Regulatory exam mandates elevated risk classification
- External intelligence (law enforcement, sanctions updates) requires immediate risk elevation

### Mechanism:
- Individual customer override via UI (select customer → override → justification)
- Bulk override via **Excel upload** for target populations
- Justification is **mandatory** — cannot override without providing reason

## Interview Notes
- Override is permanent — critical design decision for AML compliance
- Customer-level override (not account-level) aligns with overall CRR strategy
- FIU and MCO are the primary users of this capability
