# 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level

**BRD Ref:** 12.4
**Status:** Yes (Rule Execution Team)
**Beads Task:** career-context-jmh

## Business Requirement
System should calculate the Risk Rating for each unique Customer ID.

## Purpose
Enable the calculation of risk at the customer level.

## Outcome
Consistent view of the customer risk in the ecosystem; taking into consideration all the variables that influence the risk assessment.

## Key Architecture Decision (from Coaching)
- **Final Customer Risk Score** = max(Pure Customer Score, Max Hierarchy Customer Score) — this is the ONLY score exposed downstream
- Account-level risk score = internal intermediate only (used in max hierarchy logic) — being **decommissioned** from all downstream platforms
- Downstream systems receive Final Customer Risk Score — never Pure Customer Score, never Account-level
- Legal Entity level uses **Obligor ID**

## Score Hierarchy Exposed Externally
```
Account Score (internal only — being decommissioned)
  └── Pure Customer Score (internal calculation)
  └── Hierarchy Customer Score (max of account scores → max of hierarchies)
       └── Final Customer Risk Score = MAX(Pure, Hierarchy) ← THIS is what goes downstream
```
