# 12.4.6 — Automatic "Prohibited" Classification

**Parent:** 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level
**Status:** Yes (configurable flags on Risk Elements)

## Requirement
System should set CRR to "Prohibited" for:
1. If the customer/business entity address is in a comprehensively sanctioned country (North Korea, Cuba, Iran, Syria)
2. If the business entity has a prohibited company structure

Rationale set by system based on scenario.
