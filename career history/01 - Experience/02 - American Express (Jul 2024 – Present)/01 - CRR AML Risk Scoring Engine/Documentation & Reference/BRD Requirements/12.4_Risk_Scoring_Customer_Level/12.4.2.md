# 12.4.2 — Account-Level Rating from Customer Rating

**Parent:** 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level
**Status:** Not Built (Target 26.3 — DAM output)

## Requirement
If any interfacing/downstream application requires account level risk rating, the system must leverage the Customer level risk rating.

## Key Concept
The customer score is the source of truth. Account-level is derived, not independently calculated. Downstream systems (onboarding, EDD, investigations) consume via API or batch.

## Confirmed Details (from Coaching)

### Strategic Direction — Account Score Decommissioning:
- **Final Customer Risk Score** (max of Pure Customer vs Max Hierarchy) is the ONLY score exposed to downstream systems
- Account-level risk score is an **internal intermediate** — used only within the 3-tier roll-up calculation
- Account-level score will be **decommissioned** from all downstream platforms currently consuming it
- No downstream system should receive or depend on account-level risk scores going forward

### What This Means:
| Score Type | Exposed Downstream? | Purpose |
|-----------|---------------------|---------|
| Account Risk Score | NO — being decommissioned | Internal intermediate for hierarchy calculation |
| Pure Customer Score | NO | Internal — most risky elements across all accounts |
| Hierarchy Customer Score | NO | Internal — max account → max hierarchy |
| **Final Customer Risk Score** | **YES — the only score sent downstream** | MAX(Pure, Hierarchy) — single source of truth |

### Downstream Consumption:
- Downstream systems (EDD, Investigations, Onboarding, etc.) will consume Final Customer Risk Score
- Delivery mechanism: DAM output (Target 26.3)
- Previously account-level scores were consumed — migration to customer-level is underway

## Interview Notes
- Account score decommissioning is a deliberate strategic move, not a gap
- Final Customer Risk Score = MAX(Pure Customer, Max Hierarchy) — this is the only externally visible score
- DAM will provide this output once built (Target 26.3)
