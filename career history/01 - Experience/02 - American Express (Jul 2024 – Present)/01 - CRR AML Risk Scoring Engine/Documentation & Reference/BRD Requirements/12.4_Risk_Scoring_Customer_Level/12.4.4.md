# 12.4.4 — Risk Rating to Risk Class Mapping

**Parent:** 12.4 Risk Scoring - Customer Level
**Status:** Yes

## Requirement
System must have a mapping between:
- **Risk Rating** (1-10 scale, normalized from Score)
- **Risk Class** (Low, Medium, High)

## Key Concept
The raw score is converted to a 1-10 rating, which is then classified into risk classes for downstream consumption.
