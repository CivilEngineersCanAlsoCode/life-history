# 12.1.1 — CRR Risk Categories

**Parent:** 12.1 CRR Structure and Setup
**Status:** In Development

## Requirement
The system should have the below Risk Categories:
- **Products/Services** — Product types, channels, acquisition methods
- **Customer** — Customer profile, KYC attributes, PEP status, beneficial ownership
- **Geography** — Customer location, transaction locations, FATF ratings, sanctions
- **Transaction** — Velocity, amounts, patterns, cross-border activity
- **ARFs/HROs** — Additional Risk Factors & High Risk Obligors

## Key Facts (from CRR Methodology 2021)
- Categories are **FIXED** — markets cannot create their own custom categories
- BRD originally listed "Events" but this was Infosys consultants' interpretation — actual 5th category is ARFs/HROs
- **ARF (Additional Risk Factors):** 3 levels (1, 2, 3) — cumulative impact on score
- **HRO (High Risk Obligors):** Always sets customer score to 9 (High Risk) regardless of calculated score
- In CRR 2.0, ARFs/HROs are integrated as configurable risk elements within the framework (vs. separate external process in Cadence)

## Scoring Hierarchy
```
Framework
└── 5 Risk Categories (fixed)
    └── 40+ Risk Elements (within each category)
        └── Rulesets
            └── Individual Rules
```

## Formula
- **Risk Element Score** = Weighting Value × Multiplier
- **Category Score** = Sum of all element scores in that category
- **Customer Score** = Sum of all category scores
- **3-tier roll-up:** Account → Hierarchy → Customer

## Risk Classes (Enterprise Thresholds)
| Rating | Score Range | Risk Class |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| 1-3 | Low threshold | Low |
| 4-6 | Medium threshold | Medium |
| 7-9 | High threshold | High |
| 10 | Prohibited triggers | Prohibited |

- Enterprise thresholds: 10 boundaries (0 to ∞)
- Markets can override thresholds locally
- **80/10/10 rule** used for threshold determination

## Discovery Questions (Answered)
- **Should new categories be creatable by Markets?** → NO. Fixed at 5.
- **Should Risk Categories be fixed or can markets define their own?** → FIXED. Markets can only customize elements/weights within these categories.
- **Does the hierarchy need to be hardcoded or configurable?** → Categories are hardcoded; Elements and below are configurable.

## Production Data Analysis (from Prod_Assessment_Complete_Rule_Details)

### Element & Rule Distribution by Category
| Category | # Elements | # Rules | Notable Observations |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| ARFs/HROs | 13 | 309 | Weight 4000 = auto High Risk (score 9) |
| Customer | 32 | 2,199 | KYC, PEP, beneficial ownership rules |
| Geography | 28 | 1,439 | FATF ratings, sanctions, country risk |
| Products/Services | 1 | 31 | Only FA-based element; product risk is static/classificatory |
| Transactions | 42 | 7,962 | Cross-border spend alone = 689 rules |
| **TOTAL** | **116** | **11,941** | Across 40+ markets |

### Key Insights from Production Data

**Why Cross-Border Spend has 689 rules (most of any single element):**
- FATF, FinCEN, EU AMLD all classify cross-border transactions as "inherently high-risk"
- Multiple jurisdictions = regulatory arbitrage opportunity for criminals
- Correspondent banking chains (3-7 intermediary banks) obscure transaction visibility
- Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML) accounts for ~87% of global illicit financial flows ($800B-$2T/year)
- Rules must cover: country pairs, amount thresholds, frequency patterns, sanctions lists, FATF ratings

**Why Weight 4000 = Automatic Score 9 (High):**
- HRO (High Risk Obligor) elements use weight 4000 as a hard override
- Bypasses normal weighted scoring formula entirely
- Directly sets customer risk score to 9 (High Risk) regardless of calculated score
- Enterprise thresholds: 1-3 Low, 4-6 Medium, 7-9 High, 10 Prohibited
- 80/10/10 rule used for threshold determination

**Why "Repat" prefix elements exist (Weight=0):**
- "Repat" = Repatriated EU centers — post-Brexit migration from AESEL (UK) to AEESA (Spain)
- Brexit (Jan 2021) eliminated EU passporting rights for UK-based entities
- Amex migrated EU operations from UK entity (AESEL) to Spain entity (AEESA)
- Weight=0 means element is deactivated for that entity's regulatory context
- 440+ financial firms relocated from London to EU; £900B+ in bank assets moved

**Why Products/Services has only 1 element (31 rules):**
- Product risk is **static/classificatory** (credit card type doesn't change daily) vs. transaction risk which is **dynamic/behavioral**
- Product risk captured through **Fundamental Assessment (FA) PRR gate** — a questionnaire-based scoring system (10 ranked questions, score 1-10)
- The 31 rules are mapping rules connecting product types to FA scores
- This is design by choice: product risk is a classification problem, not a pattern detection problem
