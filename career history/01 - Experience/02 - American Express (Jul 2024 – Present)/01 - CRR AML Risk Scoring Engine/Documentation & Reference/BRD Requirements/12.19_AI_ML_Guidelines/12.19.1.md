# 12.19.1 — AI/ML Implementation Considerations

**Parent:** 12.19 AI/ML Migration Guidelines
**Status:** Not Started (2027)

## Requirement
POA state could consider moving to an AI/ML based model. Key considerations:

### 1. Regulatory Compliance and Transparency
- **Explainability:** Solution must be interpretable — provide detailed explanation of how risk rating was generated
- **Compliance:** Must meet regulatory standards including guidelines on fairness, accountability and transparency

### 2. Data Quality/Availability
- Data challenges in BRD must be solved as pre-requisite
- Historical CRR data, customer profiles and past risk outcomes required for training
- Data must cover all customer types and risk scenarios to avoid bias/inaccuracy

### 3. Integration
- Must integrate with existing applications (onboarding, AML, TM) for ongoing data ingestion

### 4. Ongoing Monitoring
- Model must be continuously reviewed for performance, accuracy and fairness
- Retraining/recalibrating may be needed based on changes in customer behavior, market conditions, or regulations

## Coaching Notes
- Explainability requirement aligns with current sandbox approach — CRR tab already shows full framework transparency
- Data quality pre-requisite directly maps to 12.17 (all 6 sub-requirements)
- Integration requirement maps to existing GCP architecture: DAM, GCIP, LUMI, PubSub, BigQuery
- Ongoing monitoring would extend 12.18 (Performance Reporting) to ML model metrics (accuracy, drift, fairness)
- The 4 considerations in this requirement are industry-standard for ML in regulated environments (AML/KYC)

## Interview Notes
- Not Started — Target 2027
- These are directional guidelines, not implementation specifications
- Current rule-based system provides a baseline for comparison when evaluating ML model performance
