# BRD 12.13.3 — Risk Distribution Dashboard

## Parent Requirement
12.13 — Reporting / Dashboards

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
CRR Risk Distribution dashboard in GCIP (source of truth). Data sent to LUMI for additional stakeholders. Dynamic. Configurable by Enterprise/Center/Market/Product/Legal Entity. As-of reports.

## Key Details
- Primary dashboard hosted in GCIP (source of truth)
- Data sent to LUMI for additional stakeholder access
- Dynamic and interactive dashboard
- Configurable by Enterprise, Center, Market, Product, and Legal Entity
- As-of reports (point-in-time snapshot)

## Coaching Notes
- Maps to CRR Reports **R9** (Market-level comparison) and **R10** (Product-wise comparison)
- R9/R10 show Production vs Sandbox risk distribution — similar to 12.9.5 sandbox analysis dashboard
- Dashboard grouped by: Center × Product × Legal Entity × Risk Score
- Metrics per row: accounts, hierarchies, customers, obligor IDs
- Risk bands: Low (1-3), Medium (4-6), High (7-9), Prohibited (10)
- GCIP = source of truth, LUMI = extended access for additional stakeholders via BigQuery pipeline
