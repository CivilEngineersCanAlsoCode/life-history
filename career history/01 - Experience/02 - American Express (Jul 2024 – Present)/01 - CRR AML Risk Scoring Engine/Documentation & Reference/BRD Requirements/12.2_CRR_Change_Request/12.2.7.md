# 12.2.7 — Notification on Submission

**Parent:** 12.2 CRR Change Request
**Status:** Not Started

## Requirement
Once a request is submitted, system must send a notification to the CRR team.

## Current Process (SharePoint Baseline)
- **Today:** No automated notification — CRR team (GFCC-RMO) manually checks SharePoint list for new submissions
- **Assignment:** "Assigned To" column updated manually by CRR team after they notice new entries
- **Gap:** Delay between submission and team awareness; no SLA tracking on response time

## What 12.2.7 Aims to Digitize
- Automated notification to CRR team distribution list on submission
- Notification should include: Request ID, Requestor, Market, Modification Type/Category, Summary
- Channel options: Email notification, in-platform alert, or both
- Foundation for SLA tracking (time from submission to first review)
