# 12.2.11 — Audit Logging

**Parent:** 12.2 CRR Change Request
**Status:** Not Started (Medium priority — may be de-scoped)

## Requirement
All change requests and the underlying actions taken for each must be logged for audit purposes.

Note: Based on review of new TMRPO process, this requirement may be updated/de-scoped.

## Current Process (SharePoint Baseline)
- **Today:** SharePoint provides basic metadata: Created, Modified, Created By, Modified By
- **No structured audit trail:** Cannot track who changed which column, when, or what the previous value was
- **Manual notes:** "GFCC-RMO Notes" column used for ad-hoc tracking of actions taken
- **Compliance gap:** For AML/CFT regulatory purposes, a complete audit trail of change governance is expected

## What 12.2.11 Aims to Digitize
- Immutable audit log capturing every action on every change request:
  - Who submitted, when
  - Every status transition with timestamp and actor
  - Every comment/note added
  - Document uploads/downloads
  - Withdrawal events with reason
- Queryable audit trail for regulatory/compliance reviews
- Export capability for exam/audit responses

## Priority Note
- Medium priority — may be updated or de-scoped based on review of new TMRPO (Technology & Model Risk Process Office) process
- If TMRPO provides alternative governance tracking, this may be simplified or removed
- Core audit capability may already be partially covered by platform-level logging
