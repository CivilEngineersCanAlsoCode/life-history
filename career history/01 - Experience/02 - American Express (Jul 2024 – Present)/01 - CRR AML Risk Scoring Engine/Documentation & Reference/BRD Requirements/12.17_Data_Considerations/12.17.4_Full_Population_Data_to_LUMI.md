# BRD 12.17.4 — Full Population Data to LUMI

## Parent Requirement
12.17 — Data Considerations

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
Full population data (down to account level) must be available to LUMI for analysis outside GCIP. Include all historical data.

## Key Details
- Full customer population data available to LUMI
- Data granularity down to account level
- Enables analysis outside of GCIP
- Must include all historical data
- Supports analytical and investigative use cases

## Coaching Notes
- Pipeline: GCIP → BigQuery → LUMI
- Maps to CRR Report **R17** (raw data extract for downstream consumption)
- Account-level granularity required even though customer-level is the strategic scoring direction (12.4)
- Historical data essential for trend analysis and regulatory examination
