# BRD 12.15.2 — Customer Factor Contribution View

## Parent Requirement
12.15 — Customer Level CRR View / Report

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
Users view customer risk rating (permission-based) and zoom into underlying factors (risk category, risk elements, points, overrides). Each factor (Product X, Industry Y for Account Z) and its contribution. Include manual overrides with details. Objective: transparency into risk rating designation.

## Key Details
- Permission-based access to customer risk rating view
- Drill-down into underlying factors:
  - Risk Category level
  - Risk Element level
  - Points assigned
  - Overrides applied
- Factor-level contribution visibility (e.g., Product X, Industry Y for Account Z)
- Manual override details included (what/who/why/when)
- Primary objective: full transparency into how risk rating is designated

## Coaching Notes
- Scoring hierarchy from 12.4: Account Score → Pure Customer Score → Hierarchy Score → Final = MAX(Pure, Hierarchy)
- Factor drill-down must show: which Risk Category contributed, which Risk Element triggered, what points were assigned, what weighting applied
- Manual overrides (12.4.3) visible with FIU/MCO justification details
- Pre-requisite: Rule Execution Team must capture factor-level scoring breakdown (not just aggregate score)
