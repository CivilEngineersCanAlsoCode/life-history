# BRD 12.15.1 — Customer Risk Rating Change Audit

## Parent Requirement
12.15 — Customer Level CRR View / Report

## Status
Not Built

## Requirement
System must track/audit all updates that resulted in customer level risk rating changes.

## Key Details
- Full audit trail of all updates affecting customer risk rating
- Track every change that results in a risk rating modification
- Supports compliance and investigation needs

## Coaching Notes
- Maps to CRR Report **R14**: Audit trail for all risk point changes per customer (user, timestamp, status, comments)
- Framework-level audit already built (12.7) — this extends audit to individual customer scoring changes
- Must capture: framework changes that affected the customer, data changes that affected scoring, manual overrides
- Critical for regulatory examination and investigation workflows
